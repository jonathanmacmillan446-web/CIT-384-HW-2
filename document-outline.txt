How the structural tags are used
---------------------------------

header (role="banner")
Top-level header for the whole page. Contains the H1/H2 titles and the nav.
Note: <article> also has its own (unlabeled) <header>, used just to hold that
section's H3 title and intro paragraph - a locally-scoped header, not the page banner.

nav (role="navigation")
Wraps the site's link list inside the page <header>, marking it as the page's primary navigation.

main (role="main")
Wraps the article and marks it as the page's main content, distinct from the
header, nav, and footer around it.

article (role="article")
Wraps the entire lecture content. It represents a self-contained piece of content that could stand on its own.

section
Used four times inside the article, one per topic: the intro ("Conveying meaning through structure"), "Sectioning elements", "Document outlines", and "WAI-ARIA Roles". 
Each section has its own <h3> heading and breaks the article into distinct thematic chunks.

aside (role="complementary")
Nested inside the "Document outlines" section. Holds the W3C Warning blockquote - content that is related to but separate from the main flow of that section.

footer (role="contentinfo")
Page-level footer, outside of main. Holds the CSUN address/contact info for the site.
